Help

Play with friends

On Home, press Create room and send the code. Friends paste it into Join with a code. In a match, P copies the code again.

Teams

In team modes the Squad panel lists both teams. Anyone can switch their own side; the host can move anybody.

LAN play

Run the included server (npm start) and open its address on every computer. LAN connects automatically.

Phones

Turn the phone sideways. Left thumb moves, right thumb looks; Fire, Aim, Jump and Build sit under your right thumb.

If a connection stalls

Cancel on Home and try again. Some networks block the public relays; LAN play always works on the same network.
